What we do

Three services that work on their own or together.

AI automation and setup

We look at where your week goes, then set up assistants to take on the repetitive parts. They work inside the email, calendar and social accounts you already have.

  • Email sorted, with replies drafted for you to approve
  • A morning report of what came in and what needs you
  • Enquiries, quotes and bookings followed up on time
  • Website and listings kept up to date
  • A handover session so you know how it all works

Social media management

Regular posts that sound like you, without you having to think of something to say at nine at night.

  • A posting plan built around your business and the season
  • Posts written in your voice, ready for you to approve
  • Scheduling across Facebook, Instagram and TikTok
  • A short monthly note on what worked and what didn't

Customer service training

Practical sessions for the people who deal with your customers, built on four decades in hospitality and events. The approach is simple: solve and soothe.

  • Workshops for front-of-house, phone and email teams
  • Handling complaints calmly and putting things right
  • Knowing when to listen and when to speak
  • Online, or in person by arrangement
  • Led by David, who holds a CIPD Level 5 in Learning and Development

Common questions

Things people ask first.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can use email, you can use what we set up. We explain everything in plain English and show you how it works before we hand it over.

Will AI send things to my customers without me seeing them?

Not unless you ask it to. Everything starts in draft-only mode: the assistant prepares the reply or the post, and you approve it. You decide later whether anything is safe to send on its own.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we start with a free chat and then give you a quote before any work begins.

Do you only work with local businesses?

No. We're based in the Forest of Dean but most of the work is done remotely, so we work with small businesses anywhere in the UK. Training can be online or in person by arrangement.

Can I have just one of the three services?

Yes. Each one stands on its own. Plenty of businesses start with one and add another later.
