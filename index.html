About us

The Webb and the Page.

Rosy was the first female president of the UWE Photography Society and spent five years working in events before moving into marketing and creating architectural and construction video and photography. She is the creative force and voice of Webb Page Ventura.

Common questions

Things people ask first.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can use email, you can use what we set up. We explain everything in plain English and show you how it works before we hand it over.

Will AI send things to my customers without me seeing them?

Not unless you ask it to. Everything starts in draft-only mode: the assistant prepares the reply or the post, and you approve it. You decide later whether anything is safe to send on its own.

What does it cost?

There are packages from as little as £100 a month. A chat usually identifies any areas where we can help, and our advice is free.

Do you only work with local businesses?

No. We're based in the Forest of Dean but most of the work is done remotely, so we work with small businesses anywhere in the UK. Training can be online or in person by arrangement.

Can I have just one of the three services?

Yes. Each one stands on its own. Plenty of businesses start with one and add another later.
