The Boxing Day menu

The best part of Christmas is the leftovers. Two family dishes, cooked properly.

From the Punjabi pot

Turkey Tari Wala

ਤਰੀ ਵਾਲਾ ਟਰਕੀ

"tari wala" · home-style turkey in a rich gravy

Christmas turkey slow-cooked Punjabi style: onions browned low and slow, ginger, garlic, green chilli and garam masala, finished in a loose, spiced gravy for mopping up. Served with rice and warm rotis.

  • Spiced, not fierce
  • Rice & roti

From Grandma James' kitchen

Bubble & Squeak with Wild Boar Sausages

Grandma James' recipe

Crisp-edged cakes of Christmas potatoes, sprouts and cabbage, fried in the pan until they bubble and squeak. Topped with fat boar sausages and a proper onion gravy.

  • Boar sausages
  • Onion gravy
