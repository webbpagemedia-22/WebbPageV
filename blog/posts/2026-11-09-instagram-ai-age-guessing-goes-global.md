Meta is extending its AI age-estimation system on Instagram to every country. The aim is to spot people who are probably teenagers even when they signed up with an adult birthday, and move them into Teen Accounts, which carry tighter protections.

The AI looks at the whole profile rather than just the birthday field. Meta says it considers posts, comments, bios and captions, such as a mention of a school year, and also visual clues in photos and videos. Accounts it flags get a stricter experience: they are private by default, harmful comments are hidden, and direct messages are limited to people they already follow or know.

The timing follows a string of lawsuits over harm to young people, including settlements Meta agreed in August. TechCrunch says a similar rollout is continuing on Facebook, with Threads due later this year. The article calls the expansion worldwide but does not spell out UK details, so check your own account settings rather than assuming.

## What this means for your business

If your business sells to young people, such as a gym, café, tutor or clothes shop, some teenage followers may soon be unable to message you first, and your posts may reach them differently. Use your phone's insights to see whether your audience shifts, and keep a second route, such as a contact form or email address, so customers can always get in touch.
