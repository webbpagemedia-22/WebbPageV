If you use the ChatGPT desktop app, its layout is about to change. OpenAI has told users it is merging the separate Chat and Work tabs into a single experience, according to TechCrunch.

The idea is that you can start with a rough thought and then let ChatGPT carry it through without switching screens. You can bring in files and plugins, and ask it to build a website or a presentation, or to take actions on the web, while you review and steer what it does. The coding tool, Codex, will stay as a separate part of the app.

One detail matters for your budget. ChatGPT and Codex will draw on the same pool of usage allowance. OpenAI says most people won't notice, but depending on how much you use it and which model you choose, some may hit their limit and have to wait for it to reset more often.

The change will go first to a select group of users for feedback before a wider rollout. TechCrunch gives no dates and doesn't say which plans are included, and its report rests on OpenAI's own email, so details may shift. TechCrunch also notes that Anthropic merged Claude's chat and Cowork features into one interface in September.

## What this means for your business

When ChatGPT can act on your behalf rather than just answer questions, check its work before anything goes out under your name, especially anything customer-facing. Keep an eye on your usage limits too, as a busy day could leave you waiting at a bad moment.
