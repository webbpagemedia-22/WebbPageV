If your business website lives on Squarespace, this one is worth a glance. TechCrunch reports that the website builder and host is laying off about 19% of its global workforce, which the company puts at around 300 people. The Verge reported it first, and a Squarespace representative confirmed it.

Squarespace describes the move as restructuring: simplifying how it runs, sharpening its priorities and moving faster ahead of its next phase of growth. It does not say AI is the cause.

TechCrunch does, however, point to the wider backdrop. New "vibe-coding" tools, which generate a whole website from a few lines of plain English, have shaken up the market, and rivals have been buying or funding them. Squarespace has its own AI efforts, including a tool called Blueprint and a new platform called Forma, which currently has a waitlist.

What the report doesn't say is whether customers will notice any change, which teams are affected, or when the cuts take effect. So there is no sign yet that your site or support is at risk, and no need to panic.

## What this means for your business

Nothing needs to change today, but it is a good moment to make sure you own your domain name, keep a backup of your text and images, and know where your site's login details are. If you have been wondering whether an AI-built site is good enough, treat it as a starting point and have a person check it before customers see it.
