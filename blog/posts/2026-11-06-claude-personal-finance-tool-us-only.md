Anthropic has launched a Personal Finance tool for its Claude assistant. You connect your bank and credit card accounts, then ask questions in plain English. Claude sorts your spending into categories, flags things like duplicate charges, unexpected fees and bills that keep creeping up, and can explain terms such as APR or estimate how much interest you paid over the past year.

Access is read-only, so it cannot move, manage or invest your money. Accounts link through a third-party service called Plaid, and Claude can see balances, credit limits and up to two years of transactions. Anthropic says chats in the Personal Finance hub are not used to train its models, and that there are no adverts or sponsored product suggestions.

For now it is a beta for Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers in the US. TechCrunch's report does not mention the UK, so there is no confirmed date for British users. It follows similar tools OpenAI launched for ChatGPT in May.

## What this means for your business

If you are a sole trader, this shows where AI is heading: spotting forgotten subscriptions and odd charges without you scanning a statement. Nothing here is available in the UK yet, so don't expect it to replace your accountant or bookkeeping software. When something like it does arrive, check what data it can see and where it is stored before you connect a business account.
