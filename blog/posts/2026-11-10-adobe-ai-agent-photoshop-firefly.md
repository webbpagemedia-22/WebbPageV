Adobe has used its MAX event to announce a wave of AI features across Photoshop, Premiere, Lightroom and its Firefly app. The headline item is an AI agent that Adobe says will be proactive and always on, learning more about what you are working on so it can offer quick actions, such as removing a background or making a video thumbnail.

Other additions include a relighting tool in Photoshop, object removal for video in Premiere, and a Firefly tool that edits one part of an image without disturbing the rest. Firefly is also getting a tool for making on-brand graphs, and a prototyping app called Builder, which is in private beta.

Adobe is also releasing a local MCP, a connector that lets chatbots such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini use more features inside its creative apps. TechCrunch notes that Adobe is still deciding exactly what to expose, and that one Figma co-founder has called MCP an imperfect standard.

The article does not mention pricing, plans or UK availability, and gives release dates for few of the features.

## What this means for your business

If you make your own social posts, flyers or short videos, these tools could save time, but nothing here is confirmed for UK customers or for your subscription yet. Wait for Adobe's own announcement of what is included in each plan before paying for anything. Whatever tool you use, check every AI-edited image before it goes public.
