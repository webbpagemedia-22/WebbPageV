More people are asking a chatbot instead of "Googling it", and OpenAI now has a number to put on it. At a press event on Monday, Adam Fry, OpenAI's consumer product lead for ChatGPT, said the volume of searches run through ChatGPT equals about 20% of the volume run through Google, according to The Verge.

OpenAI counts a search as any question that triggers a web lookup, rather than something the chatbot can answer from memory, such as defining a word. Fry didn't say where the Google figure came from, so treat the 20% as OpenAI's own claim. A fifth is still a long way behind Google, but it is far from nothing.

The Verge also reports that OpenAI sees advertising as a route to profit. It recently began showing images of sponsored products and services in ChatGPT, and Fry said ads haven't caused a lasting backlash. Meanwhile Google keeps sending fewer visitors to third-party websites as it adds its own AI answers.

## What this means for your business

Some of your future customers will find you by asking a chatbot, not by scrolling a list of links. It is worth asking ChatGPT a few questions about your trade and area, to see whether your business appears and whether what it says is correct. Keep your Google Business Profile and website details accurate and up to date, so that whatever a chatbot finds about you is right.
