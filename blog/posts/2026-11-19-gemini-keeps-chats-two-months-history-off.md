If you use Google's Gemini app and switch chat history off, you might assume your conversations vanish almost straight away. That is about to change.

Google is extending how long it holds on to temporary chats, and chats made with history turned off, from 72 hours to around two months. The change is rolling out worldwide in the Gemini app over the coming weeks.

Google says the longer window is for safety and security, to help it spot and investigate abuse as Gemini becomes more capable. A spokesperson said this stored data will not be used to train its AI models or improve other products. Google did not say whether it changes who can look at those chats.

There is one welcome change too. The single "Keep Activity" setting is being split in two: one switch for keeping your chat history, and a separate one for letting Google use your chats to train its AI. You can now keep your history without handing it over for training. If you had Keep Activity off before, both new settings start off.

Google isn't alone. OpenAI says it may keep temporary ChatGPT chats for up to 30 days, and Anthropic keeps incognito Claude chats for 30 days by default. "Off" rarely means "gone".

Once the update reaches you, open Gemini's activity settings and check both switches.
