If the same questions land in your inbox every week, AI email templates for customer service can save you hours in the run-up to Christmas. The idea is simple: write a good answer once, then reuse it.

Start by listing the five questions you're asked most. For a shop or café that might be opening hours, delivery times, returns, booking changes and gift vouchers. Ask an AI assistant to draft a short, friendly reply to each, in your tone. Then rewrite each draft yourself and check every fact, date and policy. These become your approved answers.

Next, save them where you'll use them. In Gmail on a computer, templates are switched off at first. Go to Settings, See all settings, Advanced, enable Templates and save. Then write a reply, open the three-dot menu, choose Templates and save it as a new template. Next time, insert it with two clicks and add the customer's name and order details.

You can also ask AI to adapt an approved template to an unusual email, but keep names, addresses and order numbers out of free AI tools.

One rule matters most: a person reads and sends every reply. Gmail can send a template automatically through a filter, but that's best kept for a simple "thanks, we'll reply soon" message. It's the same approach we suggested for [replying to Google reviews with AI](https://webbpageventura.co.uk/blog/2026-10-30-reply-to-google-reviews-with-ai).

## What this means for your business

Five approved templates can turn ten-minute replies into two-minute ones, without losing your own voice. Customers get faster, consistent answers, and you stay in control of what's promised.
