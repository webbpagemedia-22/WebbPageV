Finding Christmas social media post ideas as a small business shouldn't eat into your busiest weeks. With an AI assistant and an hour, you can plan December in one sitting, then spend the month checking and posting rather than starting from a blank screen.

Start with one planning prompt. Tell the AI what you sell, who your customers are, the tone you like, and the dates that matter: last order dates, late opening, when you close and when you reopen. Ask for a four-week plan with two or three posts a week, mixing gift ideas, behind-the-scenes moments, thank-yous and practical notices. Keep customer names and anything private out of the chat.

Next, ask for drafts of the posts that must be exactly right: your holiday opening hours and last order dates. Check every date against your own diary, because AI can invent one with total confidence. If you've already set your festive auto-reply using our [Christmas out of office tip](https://webbpageventura.co.uk/blog/2026-10-29-christmas-auto-reply-and-opening-hours), use the same dates so everything matches.

Then read and edit every post before it goes out. Google's Business Profile help asks for posts that are professional, free of spelling mistakes and not auto-generated, so an untouched AI draft is a risk. Its posts tool covers updates, offers and events, and "Schedule this post" lets you pick a date and time, so your opening-hours notice can go live while you're busy serving.

## What this means for your business

An hour of planning now saves a daily scramble in December, and AI makes the first drafts quick. Keep the dates and the final say with you, and approve every post yourself.
