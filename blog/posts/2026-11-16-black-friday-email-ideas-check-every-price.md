Black Friday falls on 27 November this year, so if you're hunting for Black Friday email ideas as a small business, now is the time. An AI assistant is a quick way to get started: tell it what you sell, what the offer is and the tone you like, and ask for five subject lines and a short email. Pick the best, then rewrite it in your own voice, as we suggested for your [Christmas social media posts](https://webbpageventura.co.uk/blog/2026-11-11-christmas-social-media-posts-plan-with-ai).

The one job you should never hand to AI is the numbers. It will happily invent a "was £60" price or a "50% off everything" headline that sounds right but isn't true.

So check two things before you press send.

First, any "was" price. Trading Standards' pricing guidance, updated in April 2026, says the higher price should be one you genuinely charged recently, for at least as long as the period you're comparing against, and not undercut by cheaper prices in between. "Up to 50% off" is only fair if a good share of the range really has that discount.

Second, delivery. On 2 November the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) wrote to online retailers warning that some still hide delivery charges. Where delivery is compulsory, the total price including the minimum delivery charge must appear from the advert through to checkout. The CMA has already fined four businesses more than £5.1 million under these rules.

## What this means for your business

Let AI save you time on the wording, but treat every price, discount and delivery charge as your job to check. A truthful offer keeps you on the right side of the CMA and keeps your customers' trust.
