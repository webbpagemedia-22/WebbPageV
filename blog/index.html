One useful bit of AI news or a practical tip each day, and what it means for a small business.

    Making Tax Digital for sole traders: where AI helps and where it doesn't

    Making Tax Digital for Income Tax has started for the biggest sole traders and landlords. Here is where AI can help with the paperwork, and where you still need the software and an accountant.

  • AI tip 30 October 2026

    How to reply to Google reviews with AI without sounding like a robot

    A quick tip for answering Google reviews faster. Let AI draft the first version in your own voice, then read, edit and post it yourself.

    Christmas out of office message: let AI draft it, you approve it

    A quick tip for getting your festive auto-reply and Google opening hours sorted in one sitting, using AI for the wording and Google's own tool for the dates.

  • AI news 28 October 2026

    Nvidia releases a free AI model that turns recordings into text

    Nvidia has released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, a free, open-weight speech-to-text model that handles up to 40 languages and hour-long recordings. It is aimed at developers, but it may soon power cheaper meeting-note tools.

  • AI news 27 October 2026

    Opera's WebAI lets your browser run AI on your own computer

    Opera has announced WebAI, a new standard for running AI models inside the browser rather than in the cloud. It is an early preview, but it points to AI that keeps your data on your own device.

  • AI news 25 October 2026

    AI assistants you can text like a colleague are arriving, but check them first

    A growing crop of AI agents now live in iMessage, WhatsApp and SMS, and can book, schedule and send emails for you. Several are still in beta, so it pays to check privacy before trusting one.

  • AI news 24 October 2026

    Canva launches its own AI image model, built for posters and social posts

    Canva has released Canva Craft, its first in-house image generator. It is built to produce readable text and layouts for things like menus, posters and social posts, and it is available to all Canva users.

  • AI news 23 October 2026

    ChatGPT can now lock your chats, but it's not a safe

    OpenAI has added a PIN or Face ID lock for private ChatGPT conversations. It stops people glancing at your screen, but it doesn't change how your data is stored.

  • AI news 23 October 2026

    1Password lets AI agents log in without seeing your password

    1Password has launched a tool that lets AI agents sign in to your accounts on your behalf without ever being shown the password. You approve each login yourself.

  • AI news 22 October 2026

    PHP2Shell: is your website safe?

    PHP, the software behind most of the world's websites, has rushed out emergency fixes for two serious security holes that AI helped find. If your site runs on WordPress or similar, check it's been updated.

  • AI news 18 October 2026

    Meta backs down on terms that let it crawl business websites for AI

    Meta's new business terms seemed to let it scrape the websites of any business using its ads and business tools, and use what it found to train AI. After a backlash, it has rewritten them.
