ChatGPT Wallet lets AI agents shop and pay for you, starting in the US

OpenAI has launched Wallet, which lets ChatGPT's AI agents pick products and pay for them once you approve. It is a small US test for now, but online shops in the UK should start thinking about AI shoppers.

OpenAI has added a payment feature to ChatGPT called Wallet. It lets the app's AI agents, which OpenAI calls dots, help you choose something to buy and then complete the checkout for you.

You save a card once. When you ask your agent to buy something, it shows you the items, the total and the card it will use, and you confirm with Face ID or a fingerprint. The card is charged through a stand-in token, so neither the agent nor the shop sees the real card number. The shop gets only the delivery address and contact details it needs, and normal card protections such as disputing a charge still apply.

For now it is a limited test for some US ChatGPT Pro subscribers with US-issued Visa, Mastercard or American Express cards. There is no word on a UK launch. OpenAI says it plans to add spending limits and rules, so people could pre-approve certain kinds of purchase.

Not every retailer is keen. TechCrunch notes that Amazon blocks AI agents from shopping on its site, and OpenAI has published guidance for sellers who want to welcome them instead.

What this means for your business

If you sell online, some future customers may send an AI agent to your shop rather than browsing it themselves. Clear product names, prices, stock levels and delivery details on your site will help both people and agents. There is nothing to switch on in the UK yet, so watch for a launch here before spending money on it.
